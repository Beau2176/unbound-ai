Privacy Notice

Draft version: 2026-09-draft

Review required before launch. This is a product and legal-review draft. It is not the binding Privacy Notice. UNBOUND AI's launch gate must remain blocked until a reviewed, non-draft version is deliberately published.

1. Scope

This draft describes how the UNBOUND AI service is designed to handle personal information. The final notice must identify the legal operator, applicable jurisdictions, effective date, contact details, and any rights or disclosures required by law where the service is offered.

2. Account information

UNBOUND AI may process account information such as email address, display name, account role, plan tier, account creation timestamps, subscription state, preferences, and settings needed to provide and secure the service.

3. Conversations and user content

Messages, conversation history, research sources, citations, and other content you submit or create may be stored so the service can provide chat history, account features, data export, and continuity. Content may be sent to configured AI or research providers when needed to perform the feature you request.

4. AI and research providers

UNBOUND AI may transmit the minimum content needed to configured AI, search, or other processing providers to generate responses and perform requested features. The final Privacy Notice should identify the providers used at launch, the purposes for which information is shared, and any provider-specific retention or processing terms that apply.

5. Security and authentication data

UNBOUND AI may store password hashes, session records, device labels, security events, security alerts, passkey public-credential metadata, and hashed recovery codes. Fingerprints, face scans, device PINs, passkey private keys, and similar local authenticator secrets remain with the device or credential provider and are not stored by UNBOUND AI.

6. Operational logs and network data

The application is designed to keep structured request logs privacy-minimized. Application request logs use generated request IDs, normalized request paths, methods, status codes, and timing, and are designed not to include request bodies, cookies, query-string secrets, raw email addresses, user-agent strings, or raw IP addresses. Hosting, network, database, security, or infrastructure providers may independently process network metadata necessary to operate and protect their services; the final notice should identify relevant providers and disclosures.

7. Hard 18+ age verification

Age-restricted features may use a third-party age-verification provider. UNBOUND AI is designed to retain minimal verification state, an 18+ threshold result, timestamps, expiration state, result codes, and an opaque or hashed provider reference. UNBOUND AI is not designed to store raw identity-document images, face scans, or biometric templates from the age-verification process. The final notice must identify the selected provider and explain its independent processing practices.

8. Billing and subscriptions

If paid plans are enabled, a third-party payment processor may handle checkout and payment details. UNBOUND AI is designed to store subscription status, plan tier, billing periods, cancellation state, opaque provider customer/subscription identifiers, and webhook event metadata needed to administer access. The application is intentionally designed not to store full payment-card numbers or raw payment-method details.

9. Usage and cost metering

UNBOUND AI may record model/provider identifiers, request counts, token usage, web-search call counts, estimated cost fields, and related timestamps for service operation, abuse prevention, capacity planning, and billing administration.

10. Legal consent and administrative audit records

The service may record policy-version acceptance, timestamps, administrator actions, entitlement changes, and other audit information needed for security, compliance, account administration, and dispute investigation. The final retention rules for these records must be reviewed before launch.

11. Retention, export, and deletion

Signed-in users may be offered a data export and permanent account deletion. Deletion removes or detaches account-linked information according to the service's deletion flow. Certain operational, fraud-prevention, audit, security, tax, accounting, or legally required records may need to be retained in de-identified or limited form. Exact retention periods must be finalized before publication.

12. Service providers

UNBOUND AI may rely on hosting, database, AI, search, email, billing, age-verification, analytics, security, and other service providers. The final notice must list or appropriately describe the providers actually used at launch and the categories of information disclosed to them.

13. Security

UNBOUND AI uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encrypted transport, authentication controls, passkeys, recovery controls, rate limiting, least-privilege access patterns, privacy-minimized logging, and operational monitoring. No system can guarantee absolute security.

14. Adults-only service

UNBOUND AI is intended for people age 18 or older. The final notice must specify how reports involving underage accounts are handled and any legally required deletion or escalation process.

15. International transfers and regional privacy rights

To be completed with legal review. The final notice must address applicable regional privacy rights, lawful bases where required, international data transfers, appeal processes, authorized agents, and jurisdiction-specific notices based on where UNBOUND AI operates and where users are located.

16. Contact and changes

Operator and privacy-contact details must be completed before publication. The final Privacy Notice should include an effective date, version identifier, contact method, and a process for notifying users of material changes.
