2. Account and profile information

UNBOUND AI may process information such as email address, display name, account role, plan tier, account creation timestamps, preferences, settings, legal-consent records, subscription state, and security/account-recovery information needed to provide and protect the service.

3. Conversations, research, and user content

Messages, conversation history, research queries, source metadata, citations, preferences, and other content you submit or create may be stored so the service can provide chat history, continuity, account features, exports, and requested AI functionality. Content needed to perform a request may be transmitted to configured AI or research providers.

4. OpenAI API processing

The current production architecture is designed to use the OpenAI API for AI generation and related model capabilities. UNBOUND AI may send prompts, conversation context, mode instructions, and other information needed to perform the feature you request. The final notice must reflect the OpenAI products/endpoints actually enabled at launch, the account's retention configuration, and the then-current provider terms and privacy commitments.

UNBOUND AI should avoid sending credentials, recovery codes, raw billing secrets, raw age-verification evidence, or unrelated account data to the AI provider merely because those values exist elsewhere in the service.

5. Render hosting and Neon database infrastructure

The current full-site application and browser worker run on Render, and the PostgreSQL database runs on Neon. Account records, conversation history, security records, and other application data stored in PostgreSQL are processed by Neon; application requests and browser-worker activity are processed by Render as needed to operate those services. The final notice must reflect both providers, their actual regions, backup and recovery arrangements, and applicable data-processing terms at launch.

6. Authentication, device, and security information

UNBOUND AI may store password hashes, session records, device labels, passkey public-credential metadata, hashed recovery codes, security events and alerts, rate-limit state, and limited audit information. Passkey private keys, device PINs, and local biometric unlock data remain with the user's device/authenticator and are not stored by UNBOUND AI.

7. Operational logs and network data

The application is designed to keep its own structured request logs privacy-minimized by using generated request IDs, normalized paths, methods, status codes, and timing rather than request bodies or raw authentication secrets. Infrastructure, network, security, and provider systems may independently process network or device metadata needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and prevent abuse of their services. Final disclosures must reflect the production stack actually used.

8. Yoti hard 18+ age verification

The current production-target hard age-verification provider is Yoti Age Verification Service. UNBOUND AI sends Yoti an opaque verification reference plus the approved hosted-flow configuration. Depending on the age-checking method available in the approved Yoti template and selected by the user, Yoti or its subprocessors may process information such as a facial image, identity document, payment-card check, mobile-provider information, database-check information, or other age-assurance evidence.

UNBOUND AI is designed not to receive or store that raw evidence. The application retains a normalized verification status, the 18+ threshold, verification/expiry timestamps, a limited result code, and a hash of the provider session reference. The production template must be reviewed for the age methods offered, including an appropriate non-biometric alternative where required.

9. Segpay billing and subscriptions

The current production-target billing provider is Segpay. UNBOUND AI's hosted-checkout integration is designed to send a signed price and opaque account reference to Segpay. Users enter payment information in Segpay's payment flow, and UNBOUND AI is intentionally designed not to store full payment-card numbers or CVV values.

UNBOUND AI may retain subscription status, plan tier, billing periods, cancellation state, opaque provider customer/subscription identifiers, and normalized/idempotent webhook metadata needed to grant or remove paid access, investigate billing state, and safely handle account deletion.

10. Amazon SES account email

The current production-target transactional email service is Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon SES). If enabled, UNBOUND AI may transmit the recipient email address, limited display-name information, and the content needed for an account-verification or security email. Verification links use one-time tokens; UNBOUND AI stores only a cryptographic token hash in its database rather than the raw token.

11. Usage, entitlement, and cost metering

UNBOUND AI may record model/provider identifiers, request counts, token usage, research or web-call counts, entitlement state, estimated cost fields, and related timestamps for service operation, capacity planning, abuse prevention, product administration, and billing.

12. Legal consent and administrative audit records

The service may record policy-version acceptance, acceptance timestamps, administrator actions, entitlement changes, security actions, and other audit information needed for account administration, security, compliance, and dispute investigation. The final notice must set reviewed retention rules for these records.

13. Retention, export, and deletion

Signed-in users may be offered data export and permanent account deletion. Deletion removes or detaches account-linked information according to the service's deletion flow, subject to records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, billing/accounting, disputes, legal obligations, or other reviewed purposes. Exact retention periods are not finalized in this draft and must be documented before publication.

14. Service providers and changes

The current full-site stack includes Render for application/browser-worker hosting and Neon for PostgreSQL database storage. OpenAI is configured for AI processing; Amazon SES, Segpay, and Yoti remain the intended transactional-email, commercial-billing, and hard age-verification providers pending full-site setup and verification. Provider selection or configuration may change. The final notice must be rechecked against the providers, subprocessors, regions, contracts, and data flows actually active at launch, and material later changes must be handled under the reviewed notice/change process.

15. Security

UNBOUND AI uses technical and organizational measures intended to reduce risk, including encrypted transport, authentication controls, passkeys, recovery controls, rate limiting, privacy-minimized application logs, webhook authentication, hashed opaque provider references, least-privilege design goals, and operational monitoring. No system can guarantee absolute security.

16. Adults-only service and underage accounts

UNBOUND AI is intended only for people age 18 or older. The final policy must specify the reviewed process for reports or evidence involving an underage account, including access restriction, deletion, preservation where legally required, provider coordination, appeals, and any mandatory reporting or escalation obligations.

The final notice must address applicable access, correction, deletion, portability, objection/opt-out, appeal, authorized-agent, sensitive-data, and other regional rights; lawful bases where required; international transfer mechanisms; and any processor/controller obligations based on the actual operator and markets served.
